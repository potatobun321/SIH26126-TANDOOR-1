SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM STATEMENT DECONSTRUCTION
====================================================================================================

1.1 Operational Context & The Defense Problem
Modern defense, perimeter security, and search-and-rescue Unmanned Ground Vehicles (UGVs) operate in 
hostile, contested, or remote outdoor environments where Global Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS / GPS) 
are actively jammed, electronically spoofed, or physically attenuated by dense forest canopies, subterranean 
structures, and steep mountain ravines.

Under GPS-denied conditions, standard robotic systems suffer from two fatal failure modes:
1. Odometry Drift: Wheel slip on loose gravel, sand, mud, and uneven outdoor terrain causes rapid, 
   unbounded dead-reckoning drift (>1.75 meters error per 10 meters of travel).
2. Off-Road Traversability Blindness: Standard 2D indoor LiDAR and flat-ground costmaps treat obstacles as 
   binary (0 or 254). They fail to classify non-binary outdoor terrain—such as distinguishing an energy-efficient 
   packed dirt trail from battery-draining tall grass, dense brush, steep drop-offs, or solid boulders.

1.2 The TIKKA-NAV Solution
TIKKA-NAV is an evidence-backed, closed-loop autonomous navigation stack built on ROS 2 Jazzy and 
Gazebo Harmonic. It delivers reliable Point-A to Point-B autonomous traversal across rugged terrain relying 
strictly on onboard monocular/RGB-D camera feeds and a 9-axis industrial IMU with zero satellite dependence.

1.3 Key Architectural Highlights
• Lightweight Edge AI Perception: MobileNetV3-Small FPN (0.29 MB footprint, 3.86 ms inference).
• Calibrated IPM: Analytical ray-plane metric projection running in 36.7 µs, cutting ground error by 92.8%.
• Native C++ Costmap Plugin: TraversabilityLayer dynamically penalizing difficult terrain in Nav2.
• High-Precision EKF: Wheel + IMU fusion maintaining 1.8 cm ATE RMSE continuous drift.
• Tactical Operator Console: Browser-based Mission Control (port 8080) streaming real-time Tactical HUD video.
• Indigenized Edge Hardware: 20W NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano, 480 Wh LiFePO4 battery (6h endurance), ₹1.74L BOM.

SECTION 5: CALIBRATED INVERSE PERSPECTIVE MAPPING (IPM ENGINE)
====================================================================================================

5.1 The Mathematical Problem: Pinhole Perspective Distortion
Naive perception nodes apply linear resizing (`cv2.resize`) to camera images, assuming pixels scale linearly. 
However, pinhole camera optics scale hyperbolically with ground distance: v = cy + fy * (Hc / X).
This causes distant obstacles (3.5–5.0m away) to be projected at 1.5–1.9m in the costmap (>1.7m metric error).

5.2 Calibrated Ray-Plane Projection Formulation
For any 2D ground cell coordinate (Xg, Yg) in `base_footprint` with ground plane Z=0:
1. Offset to camera optical center: ΔX = Xg - X_mount (0.32m), Yc = Hc (0.33m), Xc = -Yg, Zc = ΔX.
2. Direct sensor projection equations:
   u = cx - fx * (Yg / (Xg - X_mount))
   v = cy + fy * (Hc / (Xg - X_mount))
3. Precomputed Lookup Table: A 60x60 matrix mapping (gx, gy) -> (u, v) is generated once at startup.
4. Real-time per-frame indexing executes in 36.7 µs (0.037 ms).

6.1 Head-to-Head Benchmark: EKF Sensor Fusion vs Pure Visual Odometry (`eval/logs/vo_report_20260912_033919.md`)
We tested RTAB-Map Visual Odometry (RGB-D feature tracking) against our 100 Hz Wheel+IMU EKF:
• Wheel + IMU EKF Drift: 1.8 cm ATE RMSE (0.018 m) | Mean Drift: 0.010 m per 10m | 100.0% continuous uptime.
• Pure Visual Odometry Drift: 1.753 m ATE RMSE | Mean Drift: 0.945 m per 10m | 34.3% uptime (yaw loss).
• Conclusion: Rapid skid-steer turns cause visual feature blurring and tracking loss. Our tightly coupled 
  IMU + Wheel EKF guarantees rock-solid, bounded continuous dead-reckoning without satellite signals.

7.2 Graded Traversal Cost Assignment
• Trail Cells (Class 1) -> Cost: 0 (Optimal energy efficiency, maximum velocity allowed).
• Grass / Light Soil (Class 2) -> Cost: 20 (Light rolling resistance penalty).

• Result: Global Dijkstra/NavFn planners automatically route the UGV along smooth dirt trails over 
  energy-sapping rough brush, maximizing battery range and traversal speed.
